Joining the organisation on GitHub.

Once you've created your GitHub account, you can join the "phoenixprojectsoftware" organisation on GitHub. The organisation is the safespace where we host all of our projects, programs, resources, and source code, be it confidential or public. In order to receive an invite, send Sabian your GitHub username in the Discord, and you will be invited. You will receive your invite in your Email Inbox, so stay tuned to that once a confirmation has been given and remember to check the spam/junk folder if it doesn't come through.
